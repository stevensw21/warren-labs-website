Privacy & classroom tips

Microphone sound is analyzed live on your device. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. No student accounts, names, scores, or tracking are used. Uploaded drawings are stored only in this browser and are not shared between devices. Clear drawings here before using a shared device. Browser storage can be cleared by the browser.

Use this as a playful group activity, never to single out a student. Sound levels are relative, not decibel readings. Microphone placement matters. Calibrate with ordinary quiet working noise, away from fans or speakers. Sudden sounds shorter than 2.5 seconds will not make a friend leave. Switching tabs stops listening; press Start listening when you return.

Allow microphone access when your browser asks. If unavailable, open in a current Safari, Chrome, or Edge browser over HTTPS and check site microphone settings. Demo mode needs no microphone. Full screen may not be available on some iPads.
